# Archived projects

Projects here are kept but not shown on the site: the work grid, case-study pages and
prerendering only read `content/projects/`.

To bring one back, move its folder into `content/projects/` and check its `order` in
`project.ts` so it lands where you want in the grid.

- `wordflow`: Wordflow (HTML, CSS, JavaScript). Cover only; no case study written yet.
- `piazza-di-trevi`: Piazza di Trevi brand book, with a full case study and a mockup cover.
